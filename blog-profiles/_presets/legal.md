## `legal` preset

> STATUS: documented stub — not built in first release

Intended for law blogs, legal commentary, compliance guides, and practitioner-facing analyses. Precision and structured argument take precedence over narrative hook.

---

### Steps that differ from `general`

| Step | Change from `general` |
|---|---|
| `outline` | Structure selection prioritizes IRAC (Issue, Rule, Application, Conclusion) and CREAC variants; angle/hook selection replaced by **stakes framing** (what is at risk, who is affected, why this rule matters now) |
| `headline` | Avoids sensationalism; prefers precision phrasing that signals the legal issue and jurisdiction where applicable |
| `opener` | Opens with the legal question or scenario rather than a narrative hook |
| `readability` | Density/comprehension audit preserves necessary legal terminology; flags unexplained jargon for a lay-reader gloss rather than removal |
| `humanizer` | Constrained: do not introduce colloquialisms that would undermine professional credibility |
| `fact-check` | Citations to statutes, cases, and regulations checked for accuracy; outdated or overruled authority flagged |
| `fact-reconcile` | Legal claims without citation are flagged as unverified assertions, not softened |

All other steps inherit `general` defaults.

---

### Framing vocabulary additions

| Concept | Term used in this preset |
|---|---|
| Angle/hook + structure selection | **stakes framing + IRAC structure selection** |
| Reader-persona pre-assessment | **practitioner vs. lay-reader audience check** |
| Density/comprehension audit | **precision and accessibility audit** |
