## `fiction` preset

> STATUS: documented stub — not built in first release

Intended for fiction blogs, serialized stories, flash fiction, and creative essay forms where craft and reader experience take precedence over factual accuracy.

---

### Steps that differ from `general`

| Step | Change from `general` |
|---|---|
| `outline` | Structure selection draws from narrative arc templates (three-act, scene-sequel, kishōtenketsu) rather than expository frameworks; angle/hook selection reframed as **narrative tension + entry-point selection** |
| `opener` | Opens with scene, voice, or image rather than a thesis or news angle |
| `readability` | Density/comprehension audit replaced by **pacing audit**: checks sentence rhythm, scene length, white space, and forward momentum rather than informational density |
| `fact-check` | **off** — factual verification does not apply to invented worlds and fictional events |
| `fact-reconcile` | **off** — no source reconciliation step for fiction |
| `humanizer` | Elevated priority: voice authenticity and idiosyncrasy are the primary craft signal; smooth-prose defaults actively resisted |

All other steps inherit `general` defaults.

---

### Framing vocabulary additions

| Concept | Term used in this preset |
|---|---|
| Angle/hook + structure selection | **narrative tension + entry-point selection** |
| Reader-persona pre-assessment | **genre-expectation and reader-contract check** |
| Density/comprehension audit | **pacing audit** |
