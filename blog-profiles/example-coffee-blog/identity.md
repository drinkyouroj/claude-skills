# Identity

## Brand
The Daily Grind — a practical home-coffee-brewing blog.

## Subject domain
Home coffee brewing: pour-over, French press, espresso, grinders, beans, water, technique. No cafe-industry business talk.

## Platform & format conventions
Self-hosted Ghost blog. Posts 800-1500 words, one hero image, inline gear links.

## Audience in one line
Home brewers who want cafe-quality coffee from gear they already own, without the snobbery.
