# Content structures

## Structures
- **Problem → Diagnosis → Fix**: a symptom (bitter, sour, weak) → the usual cause → the concrete fix. Use when: reader has a specific complaint.
- **Technique Walkthrough**: step-by-step method with numbers. Use when: teaching a method (e.g. how to pour).
- **Gear Reality Check**: what matters vs. what's marketing. Use when: a buying decision.

## Angles / hooks
- The cheap fix that beats the expensive upgrade.
- The one number everyone skips.
- "You're not bad at this; your water is."
