# Reader persona — "Sam"

## Who they are
Sam, 34, brews coffee at home every morning. Owns a basic burr grinder and a Hario V60. Not a hobbyist — just wants the cup to be good and consistent.

## What they already know
Knows what a pour-over is; has heard "bloom" but isn't sure why. Doesn't know brew ratios or water temperature targets.

## What they need from a piece
One concrete, fixable cause and a step they can do tomorrow morning. Numbers (ratios, temps, times) they can copy.

## What loses them
Snobbery, gatekeeping, gear they can't afford, theory with no action, the word "journey".
