# Voice

## Universal AI-tell rules (keep)
- No hollow connectives ("moreover", "furthermore"), no "it's not just X, it's Y" parallelisms, no abstract closing-line summaries, no filler affirmations.

## This blog's calibration (edit)
- Register: warm, plain, hands-on. Second person ("you"). Like a friend showing you at their kitchen counter.
- Banned words: "elevate", "game-changer", "barista-approved", "unlock", "next-level", "journey".
- Rhythm: short, declarative. One idea per sentence. Occasional one-line paragraph for emphasis.
- Closing-line rule: end on a concrete next action the reader can do today, not a reflection.
- Sample sentences:
  1. "If your pour-over tastes like licking an ashtray, your grind is too fine. Start there."
  2. "You don't need a $300 grinder. You need to weigh your beans."
