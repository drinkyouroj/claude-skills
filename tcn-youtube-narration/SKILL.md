# The Civic Node — YouTube Narration (Step 1 of YouTube Production)

## What This Skill Does

Converts an approved Civic Node article draft into a 5-7 minute trailer-format narration script for YouTube. The script is designed as a teaser that earns the click through to Substack — not as a standalone explainer or article summary.
