# 30-Second UGC Script Formula

UGC ads do not sound like ads. They sound like a friend telling you about a thing they bought. Match that.

## The 3-Act structure

### Act 1 — Hook (0 to 3 seconds)
Pattern interrupt. Make the viewer not scroll. Pick ONE:

- **Bold claim:** "I have not bought shampoo in 6 months and here's why."
- **Confession:** "Okay I was wrong about [category]."
- **Curiosity loop:** "I just found the weirdest thing on Amazon."
- **Direct callout:** "If you have [problem], stop scrolling."
- **Visual reveal:** Hold the product up to camera in the first frame.

The hook must show the product in the frame. Do not save the reveal for later.

### Act 2 — Demo (3 to 25 seconds)
Show the product solving a problem. Not features, problems.

Structure:
1. **Problem** (3 to 8s): "I used to [annoying thing]."
2. **Discovery** (8 to 14s): "Then I found [product]."
3. **Result** (14 to 22s): "Now I [outcome]."
4. **Proof** (22 to 25s): One concrete number, before/after, or visible result.

Show the product on screen for at least 70% of this act. Hands holding it, using it, or sitting beside it.

### Act 3 — CTA (25 to 30 seconds)
One direct ask. Not three.

- "Link is in my bio."
- "Comment YES and I'll send you the link."
- "Use code [CODE] for 20 percent off."
- "Follow me for more finds like this."

End with eye contact to camera and a half-smile.

## Strong UGC hooks (steal these)

1. "POV: you finally found the [category] that actually works."
2. "Tell me why I just did this for 30 dollars."
3. "I am never going back to my old [category]."
4. "Things that should be illegal: [product] being this good."
5. "Stop buying [expensive alternative]. Buy this instead."
6. "Trying [product] so you do not have to."
7. "If you do not own this yet you are wrong."
8. "Three things I bought this month that actually changed my life. Number one."

## Voice rules

- Talk to one person, not a room.
- Use "you" more than "I" past the first sentence.
- Drop into shortened speech. "Gonna" not "going to". "Cause" not "because".
- One idea per sentence. If you can split a sentence, split it.
- 5th grade reading level. The Hemingway Editor is your friend.
- No em dashes. Period. Comma. Or rephrase.
- Don't say "literally" or "honestly" or "let me tell you".

## Length math

Average TikTok speaking pace is around 2.5 to 3 words per second. So:
- 3-second hook = 7 to 9 words
- 22-second demo = 55 to 66 words
- 5-second CTA = 12 to 15 words
- Full script = ~80 to 90 words total

If your script is over 100 words, cut.

## Example (good)

```
[0-3s, holding product up]
"Okay I was wrong about face roll-ons."

[3-8s, mirror shot]
"I used to wake up with a puffy face every single morning."

[8-14s, applying product]
"Then my friend gave me this. It is a 12-dollar ice roller."

[14-22s, before/after split]
"Now my face is half the size in five minutes. No filter, no edit."

[22-25s, side view]
"My jaw is actually visible again."

[25-30s, eye contact, soft smile]
"Link is in my bio. Comment ICE and I'll send you the exact one."
```

Word count: 78. Fits 30 seconds at natural pace.

## Example (bad)

```
"Hey guys! Today I want to introduce you to an amazing new product
that I think will revolutionize your skincare routine. This roller
uses cold therapy technology to reduce puffiness..."
```

That sounds like an ad. Re-write until it sounds like a friend.
