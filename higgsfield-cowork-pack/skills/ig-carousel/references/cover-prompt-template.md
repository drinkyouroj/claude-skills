# Cover Prompt Template

Drop the topic into the bracketed fields. Keep everything else identical.

## Template

```
Generate a 1080x1350 portrait (4:5) Instagram carousel cover.
Photorealistic, cinematic, movie-poster quality. Warm golden/amber
volumetric light, depth of field, film grain.

SCENE: [scene description, 2-4 sentences. e.g. A late-night
architect's loft studio. Exposed warm-red brick walls, oak drafting
table, retro CRT computer showing the EXACT Claude AI app icon
(terra-cotta/orange rounded square with white sunburst/asterisk —
10+ tapered rays pinched at the tips). Banker's lamp, brass
compasses, leather journal, steaming coffee mug. Warm amber
volumetric light.]

TEXT OVERLAY:
- '[TITLE]' in massive bold italic serif (Playfair Display style),
  salmon/orange (#DA7756), upper third, all caps, slight letter
  spacing
- Just below in slightly smaller white italic serif:
  '[subtitle in plain English, 4-7 words]'

HOOK PHRASE (lower middle, above footer):
'[hook phrase, ends in "..."]' in elegant white italic serif

FOOTER: '@aifornontechies' bottom-left in white italic serif,
'save for later' bottom-right in white italic serif. Subtle dark
gradient under the footer.

No carousel dots. No emojis. No downward arrows.
```

## Fields to fill

| Field | What goes there |
|---|---|
| `[scene description]` | The cinematic world. Pulled from the scene table in SKILL.md. |
| `[TITLE]` | The name of the thing, all caps. e.g. LIVE ARTIFACTS, CLAUDE SKILLS, THE NEW WAY. |
| `[subtitle]` | Plain-English clarifier. e.g. "Claude's hidden superpower", "the 7 I use daily". |
| `[hook phrase]` | The curiosity loop. e.g. "the page that updates itself...", "you're going to want this...". |

## Rules

- Title in salmon. Subtitle in white. Hook in white.
- Hook phrase MUST end in "..." to signal a swipe.
- Never claim a number on the cover unless the carousel actually delivers that number (no clickbait).
- NEVER use em dashes in the title, subtitle, or hook.

## Failed cover patterns to avoid

- "TOP 10 [thing] FOR [year]" — boring, no curiosity loop
- "How to [thing]" — instructional, doesn't pop on the feed
- "[Number] [things] you need" — listy without earning the swipe
- Anything with the word "ultimate" or "comprehensive"
