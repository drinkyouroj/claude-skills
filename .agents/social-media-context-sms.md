## Voice & Tone

- **Four-word description**: Informed. Opinionated. Funny. **Scared.** (The fear explains the other three — humor has an edge because it's protective; opinions need evidence because they need to be bulletproof.)
- **Voice adjectives**: precise, dry, sardonic, deadpan, anti-corporate
- **Formality**: Semi-formal. Adapts by platform — X compressed, LinkedIn more measured, Substack full voice.
- **Humor**: Dry / deadpan / political satire in the Carlin–Stewart–Colbert tradition. Sardonic edge aimed outward, not inward. **Hard rule: never explain the joke.**
- **Phrases to use**: "Based" (compressed argument); song lyric references from songs everyone knows; words about how language works ("colloquial," "trope," "vulgar," "serendipitous"); selective alliteration.
- **Phrases to avoid**: The full AI hit-list in the voice profile (Section 3 / Q28). Highlights: "Picture this," "Not X, but Y," "Dive into," "It's important to note," "A testament to," "Navigating the complexities of," "That really hits," forced patterns of three. Also: therapy-speak unironically ("holding space," "toxic positivity," "somatic"), corporate jargon, "Tomorrow's X, today," "The Amazon of Y."
- **Punctuation philosophy**: Semicolons as conjunctions (not em dashes as interruptions). Spaced em dashes only, used rarely. Parentheses for comedic whispers. Profanity is a punctuation mark with mass — preserved as a scarce resource.

## Content Formats

- **Substack (long-form)**: 1,200–1,500 words, occasionally longer. Four templates exist (see voice profile Q48):
  1. **The Triple Connection** — Current Event → Historical Context → Tech/Future Implications
  2. **The System Audit** — Problem → Analysis → Solutions
  3. **The Concept Decoder** — Definition → Mechanics → Applications
  4. **The Pattern Report** — listicle
  Every template has a mandatory personal reflection section. Templates are scaffolding, not cages.
- **X**: Compressed core voice, deadpan, the Benn Eifert model — quiet setup, borrowed text does the work, never explain the joke. Short standalone posts over threads by default.
- **LinkedIn**: More polished register, same voice underneath, consciously more measured. No one-sentence-per-paragraph engagement farming.
- **Formats to avoid**: Ragebait. Pure news aggregation with no angle. Technical how-tos with no prose substance. All-lowercase run-on "performed casualness." One-sentence-per-line "schizo energy" posts. Borrowed-authority laundering ("Sam Altman literally gave a 43-minute masterclass…").

## Example Posts

### Example 1 — X (self-aware deadpan, loadout register)

> Saturday morning optimization: nicotine pouch for focus, Red Bull for energy, ARC Raiders for everything else.
> My therapist calls this avoidance. I call it a loadout.

*Why it works:* Precise specifics set up a clinical frame, then the last line reframes the whole thing without explaining the joke. Two-beat structure (setup / reframe), not three. Self-aware without being self-deprecating — the reframe is offensive, not defensive.

### Example 2 — X (political, audit structure compressed)

> Trump fired the AG who never said no to him and replaced her with the lawyer who kept him out of prison. The Justice Department is now run by someone whose entire career goal was making sure Trump faced zero consequences. Bold institutional choice.

*Why it works:* "Here's what they claim, here's what the evidence shows" compressed into three sentences. The closer ("Bold institutional choice") is deadpan understatement doing the work a paragraph of outrage would fail at. Never calls it corruption — lets the reader land there.

### Example 3 — X (one-line observation, Socratic)

> When people say "Happy Good Friday," it tells me they don't really understand what Good Friday is about.

*Why it works:* Quiet setup, the floor blows out on "what Good Friday is about" without naming the thing. The implied connection — the same move as not explaining the joke. One sentence, complete, devastating.

### Common thread across examples

- Two-beat, not three — setup + reframe, no padded middle
- Closer does the work via understatement or implication, never via explanation
- Sardonic edge aimed outward (Example 2) or at a cultural miss (Example 3); even the "inward" one (Example 1) lands offensively, not apologetically
- Zero AI-hit-list phrases. Zero therapy-speak. Zero hedging.

## Anti-Patterns

- **Topics to avoid**:
  - Ad hominem attacks on private individuals (public authority figures wielding power are fair game)
  - Gritty personal drug-use stories (professional liability, not emotional guardedness)
  - Purely factual news with no angle or point of view
- **Tones to avoid**:
  - Ragebait (attracts the wrong audience, bleeds into real life)
  - Therapy-speak deployed unironically or as a weapon
  - Bigotry, small-mindedness, stereotyping-as-argument
  - Self-deprecating humor (being consciously reduced — the internal monologue doesn't need rehearsal in public)
  - Hype framing, false scarcity, "get rich quick" energy
  - Preachy or motivational-poster tones
- **Content types to avoid**:
  - Engagement-bait polls, "agree?" one-liners
  - Public performance of private housekeeping ("unfollowed 2000 of you…")
  - Borrowed-authority posts
  - Sponsored/conflicted content without flagging the conflict
  - Hate language without clear framing that it's being critiqued rather than deployed
